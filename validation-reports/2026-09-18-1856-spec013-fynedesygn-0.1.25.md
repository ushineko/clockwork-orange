### Summary

The GUI moves from fynedesygn v0.1.2 to v0.1.25 and adopts the capabilities
the library grew over those twenty-three releases.

The bump alone is behaviour-preserving: it built and passed `make test` with
no source edit. It carries three fixes for bugs this window had — a result
banner and a hover tip were overlays and took every click in the window while
they were up (library 0.1.12, 0.1.13), a log pane drew long lines past its
right edge (0.1.16 to 0.1.19), and a banner over a log pane was unreadable
(0.1.15).

On top of the bump:

1. **Settings file** (R2): `shell.Options.SettingsPath` is
   `~/.config/clockwork-orange/gui-settings.json` — the directory this program
   already owns, beside `history.db` and `blacklist.db`. The shell migrates the
   appearance out of Fyne's preference store on first run. Nothing moved out of
   `clockwork-orange.yml`: the window size, console font and console size stay
   where 2.9.x and the daemon read them.
2. **Navigation shape** (R3): all three modes and both placements are declared,
   which raises the stock shape control in the header and binds `Ctrl+B`. The
   chosen shape lives in the settings file.
3. **Dividers** (R4): the log pane in Service and Activity sits under a bar the
   user can drag, under one shared key. The navigation's own divider comes with
   the bump.
4. **Refusal** (R5): both "Something is already running" flashes in
   `rundialog.go` are `Shell.SayBusy()`, which names the operation holding the
   indicator, offers its Cancel, and says nothing while the modal busy popup is
   already saying it.
5. **Hover tips** (R6): six controls whose consequence the label cannot fit —
   Service Install and Uninstall, History's two actions, Blacklist's Remove,
   the Appearance scale.
6. **Arrival** (R7): Blacklist and History refetch when the navigation arrives,
   not when they are rebuilt where they stand.

Diff: 9 files, +140 / -48, of which `internal/gui/app.go` and
`internal/gui/views_service.go` carry most of it.

### Phase 5.5: Release Safety

- **Rollback**: revert the commit; the previous release is installable from the
  AUR and the GitHub artifacts. The one thing a revert does not undo is the
  settings file, which a 4.1.1 binary ignores — it reads the appearance from
  the Fyne preference store, which is still there and still holds the
  pre-migration values, so the window comes back looking as it did.
- **Additive**: nothing is removed from disk and no format changes. The
  settings file is new; `clockwork-orange.yml`, `history.db` and `blacklist.db`
  are byte-for-byte unaffected, so 2.9.x and the daemon are unaffected.
- **Rollback plan**: recorded here and in the spec's "Risks & Assumptions".
